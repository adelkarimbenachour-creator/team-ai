# 🤖 Team-Ai — Autonomous AI Employees & Coworkers Platform

**Team-Ai** is an advanced platform for hiring, managing, and collaborating with autonomous AI employees. Each AI coworker operates within a dedicated virtual workstation equipped with an interactive Linux sandbox VM, an automated live browser, persistent memory, and 70+ workplace tool integrations (GitHub, Slack, Notion, Linear, Gmail, and more).

Powered by Gemini 3.7 and featuring a built-in human-in-the-loop approval system, **Team-Ai** empowers teams to safely delegate full-stack engineering, marketing growth, customer support, and scheduled background operations to reliable AI teammates.
